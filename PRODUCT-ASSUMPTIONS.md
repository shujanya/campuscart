# Product Assumptions: CampusCart

## 1. What product are you planning to build?
We are planning to build CampusCart, an online shopping platform designed specifically for college students. It allows students to browse and purchase everyday products such as snacks, drinks, stationery, essential items, and bundles at affordable prices.

## 2. What problem does it solve?
College students often face difficulties finding affordable snacks, stationery, and daily essentials within or near their campus. Visiting multiple shops takes time and effort. CampusCart solves this problem by providing a single online platform where students can easily find and purchase the products they need.

## 3. Who are the target users?
College and university students.

Students living in hostels or paying guest accommodations.

Students who regularly purchase snacks, drinks, stationery, and daily essentials.

Campus shops and small vendors who want to sell their products online.

## 4. What is the main use case?
The main use case is to help students conveniently purchase everyday products through an online platform.

For example, a student can open CampusCart, search for snacks or stationery, browse different categories, add products to the cart, and place an order without having to visit multiple shops.

## 5. What are the core features?
The main features of CampusCart include:
1. Product Listing: Displays available products with their names and prices.
2. Search Function: Allows students to search for specific products.
3. Category Filtering: Organizes products into Snacks, Drinks, Stationery, Essentials, and Bundles.
4. Shopping Cart: Allows users to add products and manage their cart.
5. Student Deals: Highlights special offers and discounts for students.
6. Order Management: Allows users to view and track their orders.
7. Stock Availability: Displays whether products are available or out of stock.

## 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?
We assume that:
1. Most students have access to smartphones or computers and the internet.
2. Students prefer affordable products and student-friendly discounts.
3. Students want a simple and easy-to-use shopping platform.
4. Students prefer saving time by ordering products online.
5. Students regularly purchase snacks, drinks, stationery, and daily essentials.
6. Students are comfortable using search, category filters, and shopping carts.

## 7. What will be included in your MVP?
The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include the basic features required to make CampusCart functional.
1. Home and product listing page.
2. Product search functionality.
3. Product categories and filters.
4. Product details with prices.
5. Add-to-cart functionality.
6. Shopping cart management.
7. Basic order placement.
8. Order history or order listing.
9. Display of student deals and product availability.

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?
The following features will not be included in the initial version but may be considered for future development.
1. AI-based product recommendations.
2. Live delivery tracking.
3. Multiple payment gateway integrations.
4. Loyalty points and reward systems.
5. Customer-to-customer selling.
6. Advanced analytics and sales reports.
7. Multi-campus expansion.
8. Voice-based product search.
